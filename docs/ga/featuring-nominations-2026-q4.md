## N1: Challenges (App Enhancements), submit now

- **Type:** App Enhancements
- **Name:** Tailspot Challenges: race your friends to catch real planes
- **Publish date:** the day 1.2.0 goes live (phased release start). If that
  date slips, edit the nomination; don't let it lapse.
- **Regions:** All (Tailspot is worldwide; ADS-B coverage is strongest in
  North America, Europe and around major airports in Asia-Pacific).

**Description:**

> Tailspot turns the planes flying over you into a game. Point your iPhone at
> the sky and Tailspot locks onto the actual aircraft you can see, using your
> position, true-north heading and live transponder data from thousands of
> community receivers. Tap it, and it's caught: type, airline, route and
> rarity, pinned to the moment you looked up.
>
> Version 1.2 adds Challenges. Start a race with up to 10 friends, choose an
> hour, a day or a week, and share an invite link. Every real plane caught
> while it's live scores, standings update as you go, and a notification tells
> you when someone passes you. It works anywhere planes fly: a backyard, a
> commute, an airport window.
>
> Every catch has to be a plane you actually saw outdoors, so the scoreboard
> means something. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases. Built by one
> independent developer with SwiftUI, Core Location and an on-device Core ML
> detector that snaps the reticle onto the plane.

- **Supplemental materials:** link to the App Store screenshot set (slide 7
  shows Challenges) and a 20–30 s screen recording of a real catch. Noah's
  field capture from slide 1 is the strongest asset.

image and a 9:16 details image (the content engine can render these).
Events appear on the product page for everyone and in search for past
downloaders, so they mainly re-engage lapsed users.

| E3 | Peak Season Freighters | Special Event | Nov 20 – Dec 20 (31-day max) | Catch the cargo jets carrying the holidays |
| E4 | Your 2026 in Planes | Major Update | Dec 1 – Dec 31 | See your year of catches, then share it |

Long descriptions (≤120):

- **E1:** Start a challenge, send the link, and race up to 10 friends to catch
  the most real planes before Sunday night.
- **E2:** Gates, windows and backyards: catch as many planes as you can during
  the year's biggest travel week.
- **E3:** A limited set of real 747, 767 and 777 freighters. Catch them before
  the holidays end.
- **E4:** Tailspot Wrapped: your rarest catch, favorite airline and longest
  streak of 2026, ready to share.

E3 and E4 depend on F5 and F4 shipping; schedule them only once the build is
