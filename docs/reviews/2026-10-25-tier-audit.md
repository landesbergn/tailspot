# Aircraft tier audit — 2026-10-25

Catalog: 2613 entries. 309 rarity changes; 187 category changes. No live catch counts or production rescore available.

## Decisions

- Approved first 34 changes, with BelugaXL and Dreamlifter Legendary. Retain existing military ladder; use Epic for civilian heritage jets. Starship and Proteus use exceptional-civilian Legendary precedent.
- Resolve unsubstantiated candidates to retain, rather than treating zero/low catches or regional absence as evidence. All remaining long-tail entries retain their current tier with low confidence; this is not a worldwide fleet census.
- Preserve existing shared civil/military designator tiers unless an exact family rule applies. Remove ineffective absent-code overrides. Do not invent typecodes or automatically turn every 747-200 into Air Force One.
- Retired/prototype status does not itself grant a higher tier; known exceptional/historical entries can have a tier without claiming they are currently catchable.
- No bonus values or points ladder changes. Backend scoring version 9 requires reference-table ingest before rescore. Dry-run production score impact before applying.
