Streak freezes, repair and forgiven days

Review of the 2026-11-13 build. The images are the real Profile screen rendered in the iOS 26 simulator from seeded catches. Nothing here has been on a phone yet.

The rules

WhatHow it works
Covered dayA day with no catch that doesn't break the streak. It holds the streak together and does not add to the count. A 12-day streak with one covered day is still 12.
FreezeEarned on every 7th streak day, 2 held at most. A missed day spends one the next time the app opens. If the freezes held can't cover the whole gap, none are spent.
RepairOffered in Profile when no freeze covered the miss. One missed day, yesterday or the day before, once every 30 days. One tap.
Forgiven dayYou declare a day for everyone with a script. The app reads the list on every open. The push is only the announcement.
Late forgivenessIf a freeze was spent on a day you forgive afterwards, the freeze is handed back.
MinimumFreezes and repairs protect a streak of 2 or more days.
Existing streaksOn first run a live streak gets the freezes it already earned: 7 to 13 days gets 1, 14 or more gets 2.

Forgiving a day

Run from backend/ against the production database. Try it with --dry-run first: it writes nothing and prints how many devices would be pushed.

npm run streak:forgive -- --day 2026-11-13 --reason "FAA ground stop" \
  --push-title "Streaks are safe today" \
  --push-body "FAA ground stop. Today won't count against your streak." \
  --min-build 121
  • Without the two push flags it only records the day. People still get covered the next time they open the app.
  • --min-build is required when pushing. It must be the first build that carries this feature, because older builds ignore forgiveness and would be told something untrue. 121 above is a placeholder.
  • --remove takes a day back. Apps drop it on their next open.
  • Running it twice with push flags pushes everyone twice.
  • Test first. --only-device <device id> in place of --min-build sends to one device only. Use a far-past day such as 2000-01-01 for the test and --remove it afterwards.

Catch reveal

Freeze earned

On a day whose first catch earns a freeze (every 7th streak day), the streak stamp shortens to 14 DAYS and adds FREEZE EARNED. It stays for every reveal that day.

Decided 2026-11-13

  • Earning a freeze gets a line on the catch reveal (above).
  • Repair stays a free tap.
  • One freeze per 7 streak days, 2 held at most.
  • The forgiven-day reason stays in the toast and push, not on the Profile card.
  • Making the push respect the streak-reminder toggle is a separate change, after testing this one.

Known limits

  • The 5pm reminder can still fire on a day you forgave after someone last opened the app.
  • Freezes and past covered days are stored on the phone only. Deleting the app loses them, like the Hangar.
  • Deploy order matters: database migration 0012, then the server, then the app.
