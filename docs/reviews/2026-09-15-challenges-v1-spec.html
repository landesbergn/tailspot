Tailspot Challenges v1

Decision-ready feature spec · 2026-09-15 · branch feat/challenges-poc (commit d132b13) · written by Claude for Noah · nothing in production has been built

Summary

Challenges let two to ten spotters race for points inside a shared time window. Anyone can start one, share a link, and the standard Tailspot score decides it. This spec turns the Codex navigation mock into a buildable v1 and lists the sixteen decisions still open.

Three things happened in this session:

  • The POC is ported. The Codex mock sat on a base 58 commits behind main. It now lives on feat/challenges-poc off today's main, builds, and runs in the iPhone 17 simulator with the same launch arguments. The unrelated Codex commit (a PLAN.md note about tar1090) was left behind on purpose.
  • The mock was reviewed against the shipped app. It is a good navigation sketch and a weak product sketch. The biggest problems: it drops the streak and points hero that v1.1 shipped, it shows an in-app invite that v1 cannot deliver without a friend graph, it has no empty, create, join, results or No Contest states, and it stacks five indicators on one fact. Details in section 1.
  • The spec is below. Where I disagree with the current direction I say so and give a recommendation. The disagreements are small: keep the bottom bar as proposed, but make the account button open the existing Profile rather than a new half-height menu, and put the live-challenge entry on the Leaders screen itself rather than only behind a toolbar flag.
What I need from you

Read section 15 first if you are short on time. D1 (account surface), D3 (late joins), D5 (cancel after start) and D8 (validation verdicts) change the build the most. Everything else has a recommendation I am comfortable defaulting to. Once you have answered, phase 0 (navigation) and phase 1 (backend) can start the same day.

1.4 Mismatches and weaknesses, candidly

  1. The account sheet loses what v1.1 shipped. The mock's half-height menu keeps identity, points and rank but drops the stats and streak card, the best catch card and the weekly / monthly champion laurels. Streaks were the lead v1.1 feature and the champion laurels are the payoff for the winner trophies. Neither has another home. A half sheet also can't host the Challenges push properly: from the account path the hub opens inside a medium detent.
  2. The On Deck invite cannot exist in v1. The mock shows "Bay Area Spotters · Invited by @eli" with a JOIN chip. Invites in v1 are links, and there is no friend graph or inbox, so the app never learns about an invite until the link is opened. On Deck can only list challenges you already joined that haven't started, plus ones you created.
  3. Five indicators for one fact. One live challenge lights a dot on the account button, a dot on the Leaders button, a dot on the toolbar flag, a "1" count badge on the Challenges row and a NEW pill beside it. Pick one live-state indicator and one first-run discovery indicator.
  4. Contrast bug. "24 PTS BEHIND @MAYA" is textSecondary on a cyan fill. It fails contrast and is nearly invisible in the screenshot.
  5. Invented HUD chrome. The mock draws a "TAILSPOT / POINT AT A PLANE" wordmark top left. The real AR view has no wordmark; that region is where the compass warning, indoor hint and toast stack. The account button is the only new top chrome the spec keeps.
  6. The leaderboard is redrawn, not reused. The real screen is a branded inset-grouped List with podium blocks, the champion banner as a List section and a Your Standing footer. The mock re-draws it as glass cards with circle numerals and bars, and titles it "Leaders" where the app says "Leaderboard". The spec reuses LeaderboardScreen as is and only adds toolbar items and one strip.
  7. No states beyond the happy path. Missing: empty hub, create form, invite share, join preflight, upcoming challenge, finalizing, results, No Contest, cancelled, offline, server-unavailable. Section 6 inventories all of them.
  8. Standings CTA duplicates the card. The full-width cyan bar inside the live card does what tapping the card does. One tap target is enough; the bar can carry the gap line instead.

What the mock gets right and the spec keeps: Hangar / Capture / Leaders at thumb height, the checkered flag as the Challenges glyph, the hub's three sections (live, upcoming, history), the aviation section voice (IN FLIGHT, ON DECK, FLIGHT LOG), mono for data and system type for prose, and glass inside a GlassEffectContainer.

2. Goals and non-goals

Goals

  • Give any spotter a competition they can win. The global board rewards the top 1%. A challenge among four friends has a real winner every time.
  • Create a reason to open the app on a specific day. A 24-hour or weekend window is a natural appointment.
  • Make invitations the first social edge. Every challenge is a reason to send a link to someone who may not have the app.
  • Keep the score honest. Standard Tailspot scoring, server-side, same as the leaderboard. No custom rules.

Non-goals for v1

  • No friend graph, follows, or in-app invite inbox. Invites are links and codes.
  • No chat, wagering, custom scoring, handicaps or team modes.
  • No push notifications. The app has no APNs foundation; adding one is a separate project. Challenge state is pulled when screens appear.
  • No offline framework. Catches already upload with retry; challenges just count what reached the server.
  • No public or discoverable challenges. You are in one because you created it or opened its link.
  • No location, photo, callsign, registration or route data ever leaves the participant's own device into a challenge view.

3.2 Refinement A: the account button opens Profile, not a new menu

The mock's half sheet is a third hub surface with a subset of Profile. I recommend the account button presents the existing ProfileScreen sheet unchanged except for one swap: the Leaders tile becomes a Challenges tile (Leaders is now on the bar). That keeps the points hero, streak, best catch and laurels, keeps Share in the toolbar, and gives the Challenges push a full-height stack. The cost is one extra scroll to reach Settings compared with the half sheet. If you prefer the half sheet, the fallback is a [.medium, .large] detent sheet whose lower half holds the rest of Profile, with the Challenges push forcing the large detent. Decision D1.

3.3 Refinement B: the live challenge lives on Leaders, not only behind a flag

A toolbar glyph is right for the cold state (you have never used Challenges). It is wrong for the hot state (you are mid-challenge and want to know if you are still 2nd). Under the WEEK / MONTH / ALL TIME switcher, when you are in a live or upcoming challenge, Leaders shows one slim strip: IN FLIGHT · Weekend Flyoff · 2nd · 48m left ›. It taps into that challenge. With no live challenge the strip is absent and the flag is the only entry. Decision D2.

3.4 Indicators

  • Discovery (first run): a cyan dot on the toolbar flag until the hub has been opened once (@AppStorage("tailspot.challenges.hubSeen")). No NEW pill anywhere else. The app has no coachmark pattern and this feature does not justify inventing one.
  • Live state: the strip on Leaders and the Challenges tile subtitle on Profile. No dots on the AR bottom bar; the AR view stays quiet.
  • Unseen result: the Challenges tile subtitle reads "Weekend Flyoff finished · see results" until the results screen has been viewed. That is the whole notification story in v1.

4. Flows

4.1 Create

1

Tap + on the hub. If the handle is unclaimed, the form is replaced by the existing claim-handle flow first (Settings → SPOTTER) and returns here.

2

Name: prefilled with a suggestion ("Tuesday Flyoff", "Weekend Flyoff") so nobody has to type. 3 to 24 characters, profanity-checked server side with the handle filter.

3

Start: Starts now (default) or Schedule with a date-time picker, at least 15 minutes and at most 14 days ahead.

4

Duration: 1 hour · 24 hours · 3 days · 7 days. Default 24 hours. The form shows the computed end in local time ("ends Wed 3:12 PM").

5

Create. The creator is the first accepted participant. The app lands on the challenge detail with the share sheet already presented.

Participant cap is fixed at 10 and not shown as a control. A head-to-head is just a challenge where two people joined.

4.2 Invite

The system share sheet with a short message and the link: Race me on Tailspot — Weekend Flyoff, 24 hours starting now. https://tailspot.app/c/K7M4QD2X. Messages renders the link as a rich preview from the landing page's Open Graph tags. The detail screen also has Copy link and shows the code in large mono so it can be read aloud at an airport fence. The link works until the challenge ends or is cancelled, and stops working when the challenge is full.

4.3 Join (Preflight)

1

Open the link (app installed) or enter the code on the hub. The Join sheet loads a preview: name, creator handle, window, who is in it, time left.

2

The rule is stated plainly in one card: "Everything you catch between Sat 9:00 AM and Sun 9:00 AM counts, including planes you already caught since it started. Standard Tailspot points. Only your handle, points and catch models are shared."

3

If the handle is unclaimed, the claim field appears inline above the Join button. If onboarding is incomplete, the invite is remembered and the sheet appears after onboarding finishes.

4

Join. Terminal states instead of the button: Full (10 of 10), Ended, Cancelled, Already in (opens the challenge), Not found.

4.4 Live

The challenge detail is the same screen in every state; the state changes what the header says and which actions exist. Live shows: countdown line, standings with shared placement, each row expandable to that spotter's catch log (model, rarity, time) and rarity breakdown, your row highlighted, participant count, Share link, Leave. Data refreshes on appear and pull-to-refresh, and every 60 seconds while the screen is frontmost. No background polling.

4.5 Results

The first time a participant opens a finished challenge, the header plays the winner moment: laurels around the winner handle (or handles on a tie), the success haptic the app already uses for catches, and a one-line verdict ("You won by 40 points", "You tied for 1st", "2nd of 5", "No contest"). After that first view it is a quiet results screen. Final standings, per-spotter catch logs and rarity breakdowns stay readable forever. Share renders a results card (handles, placement, points, window) through the same ImageRenderer path as the catch share card.

4.6 History

FLIGHT LOG lists every finished challenge you were in when it ended, newest first, with placement medal colour, "Won", "Tied 1st", "3rd of 6" or "No contest", points and date. Cancelled challenges appear greyed with "Cancelled". Challenges you left do not appear.

7. Visual design and brand conventions

  • Chrome rule. The hub, create form, join sheet and detail are utility screens: stock branded navigation, inline titles, branded inset-grouped Lists or glass cards inside one GlassEffectContainer. The results first view is the one game moment and may use the reveal vocabulary (laurels, gold).
  • Type. Mono (B612) for handles, points, countdowns, codes and ALL-CAPS section labels, always with a relativeTo: anchor inside scrolling content. System type for prose and buttons. Prose headlines use .brandDisplayFont().
  • Colour. Cyan is the accent and the "you" colour. Podium gold, silver, bronze for placement, the same tokens the leaderboard uses. Rarity tints in catch logs come from the rarity system. Never secondary text on a cyan fill.
  • Radii. 6 chip, 12 row, 16 card. Glass tinted with bgElevated.opacity(0.88) like Profile.
  • Voice. Section headers may be aviation flavoured (IN FLIGHT, ON DECK, FLIGHT LOG). Everything a person must act on is plain: Create, Join, Leave, Share link, Cancel challenge. States are plain words: Upcoming, Live, Finished, No contest, Cancelled.
  • Countdowns. Recomputed per render and by a one-minute TimelineView on the live card only, the same approach as the leaderboard's reset line.
  • Haptics. The existing sensoryFeedback(.success) on the results first view. Nothing on standings refreshes.

8. Accessibility and Reduce Motion

  • Every standings row is one accessibility element: "2nd, @noah, 380 points, 11 catches, you". Ties read "tied 1st".
  • Placement is never colour only: the ordinal text is always present next to the medal tint.
  • Dynamic Type: the stats trio on the live card and the standings row switch to a stacked layout at accessibility sizes, following the Profile stats row pattern. Codes and countdowns use mono with a text-style anchor. Snapshot both default and AX2.
  • Reduce Motion: the results first view fades laurels in instead of scaling them; no confetti exists at any setting. The live strip appears without slide. Each animated surface reads accessibilityReduceMotion at its own call site, as the app does today.
  • Hit targets: the toolbar flag, Copy link and the expandable catch-log rows are at least 44 pt, using inset content shapes where the drawn element is small.
  • VoiceOver labels for the flag: "Challenges" plus "new" while the discovery dot shows, and "Challenges, one live" when a strip exists.

9. Privacy and authorization

The boundary

A challenge participant may learn about another participant exactly: handle, total points and catch count in this window, placement, rarity breakdown, and a catch log of model name, rarity tier, points and time. Nothing else. No observer coordinates, no aircraft coordinates, no heading, no photo (none exist server side), no callsign, no ICAO hex, no registration, no route, no operator, no guess outcome, no validation verdict.

  • Who can read a challenge: accepted participants (joined, not left) with a valid bearer token. Everyone else gets 404, never 403, so codes cannot be probed for existence through the authenticated route.
  • Invite preview: a bearer route returns name, creator handle, window, participant handles and count. A separate public preview for the web landing page returns only name, creator handle, window and count, gated by the same Origin allowlist and cache as GET /v1/stats.
  • Disabled devices (devices.disabled_at) cannot create or join, vanish from standings, and their frozen results stay as they were.
  • Names pass the handle profanity filter. The creator can rename before start. Reporting is out of scope; the group is private and the creator can cancel before start or leave after.
  • Codes are 8 characters from a 31-symbol alphabet (about 40 bits). Lookups are limited per IP before any database read. Codes expire with the challenge.
  • Analytics carry challenge ids and counts, never names or codes.

10. Backend data model and API

Fastify + Drizzle + Postgres on Fly, PGlite in tests, one new migration 0010_challenges.sql applied by hand before the deploy, like every migration so far. Everything is additive; no existing table changes shape. One composite index on catches is new and also helps the leaderboard windows.

catches: ADD INDEX catches_device_caught_idx (device_id, caught_at)

Status is derived, never stored: cancelled if cancelled_at, else upcoming before starts_at, live until ends_at, finalizing until ends_at + grace, finished after finalization.

10.2 Standings query

SELECT p.device_id, d.handle,
       COALESCE(SUM(c.points), 0) AS points,
       COUNT(c.id)                AS catches,
       jsonb_object_agg(...)      AS rarity_breakdown   -- or a second grouped query
FROM challenge_participants p
JOIN devices d ON d.id = p.device_id AND d.disabled_at IS NULL
LEFT JOIN catches c
  ON c.device_id = p.device_id
 AND c.caught_at >= $starts_at AND c.caught_at < $ends_at
 AND c.created_at < $ends_at + interval '15 minutes'
WHERE p.challenge_id = $id AND p.left_at IS NULL
GROUP BY p.device_id, d.handle
ORDER BY points DESC, d.handle ASC;
-- placement computed in code: 1 + count(rows with strictly more points)

10.4 Rescoring

Live standings use current catch points, so a rescore moves a live board, which is correct. Frozen results never change, matching the weekly champion contract. The finalization writes results in one transaction and sets finalized_at; a concurrent second reader sees the row and skips.

11. Invite links, deep links and install behaviour

  • URL: https://tailspot.app/c/K7M4QD2X. One shape, no query parameters.
  • App installed: a universal link opens the app straight into the Join sheet. This needs three new things: the Associated Domains capability with applinks:tailspot.app (a manual developer-portal and Xcode step for you, like APNs would be), an apple-app-site-association file served from /.well-known/ by the nginx site with application/json, and onOpenURL in TailspotApp writing a pending invite that the catch screen presents. The catch screen's body is at the compiler's type-check budget, so the presentation rides one small ViewModifier bundled with an existing attachment point, not a new top-level chain link.
  • App not installed: Safari opens the static landing page. It reads the code from the path, fetches the public preview, and shows "@eli invited you to Weekend Flyoff · 24 hours · 3 spotters" with the App Store badge (campaign "Tailspot Challenge Invite") and the code in large type: "After installing, open Challenges and enter this code." There is no deferred deep linking on iOS without a third party, and the pasteboard trick triggers a system banner, so the code is the bridge. Join with code therefore ships in phase 2, before universal links.
  • Onboarding not complete: the pending invite waits and the Join sheet appears after the last onboarding step.
  • Link opened in Safari on a phone with the app: iOS shows the Open in Tailspot banner; the page also has an Open in app button.

Success metrics (read after the first two weeks on the App Store)

  • Share of weekly active users who were in at least one challenge. Target for a first read: 15%.
  • Catches per participant per day during a challenge against that person's prior 30-day rate. The feature works if this is above 1.5×.
  • Invite conversion: joins per created challenge, and joins per invite opened. A created challenge that never reaches two people is the failure mode to watch.
  • Completion: share of challenges that finalize as decided rather than No Contest. Below 60% means the invite loop is broken, not the game.
  • Re-challenge: share of participants who create or join another within 14 days.
  • Week-4 retention of challenge participants against activated non-participants, same cohort weeks. Lagging, read at a month.

Guardrails

  • Catch confirmation rate (north star) for catches made during a live challenge must not fall below the baseline. If it does, people are spraying captures for points.
  • Implausible verdict share among challenge catches against baseline. A rise is the cheating signal and the trigger to revisit D8.
  • catch_deleted rate during challenges.
  • Backend p95 on GET /v1/challenges/:id under 300 ms and no OOM signal on the 256 MB machine (the 2026-07-11 history).
  • Invite lookups hitting 429, as a brute-force signal.

13. Testing strategy

Backend (Vitest, PGlite, app.inject)

  • Window edges: a catch at starts_at − 1 s and at ends_at do not count; at starts_at counts.
  • Late join: catches before joined_at but inside the window count.
  • Grace: an upload with in-window caught_at and created_at at ends_at + 14 min counts; at +16 min does not.
  • Ties: 100/100/60 → placements 1, 1, 3; both 1s are winners.
  • No Contest: one participant; two participants with zero points.
  • Cancel: allowed before start, 409 after; creator leave before start cancels.
  • Leave and rejoin: row disappears then reappears with catches intact.
  • Finalization is idempotent and frozen: rescore after finalization changes live-style totals but not challenge_results.
  • Authorization: non-participant gets 404 on detail and log; disabled device vanishes from standings and cannot join.
  • Limits: 11th join gets 409; code lookup limiter returns 429 before any DB read; name profanity 422.
  • Preview route: Origin gate returns 404 for non-allowlisted origins; body never contains handles beyond the creator.

iOS (Swift Testing)

  • Decoding fixtures for every response, including additive unknown fields.
  • Pure functions: placement formatting ("1st", "T-1st", "3rd of 6"), countdown copy at 59 s / 61 min / 25 h / 3 d, code parsing from a URL and from typed input (lowercase, spaces, ambiguous characters), the challenge state machine from timestamps.

15. Unresolved decisions with recommendations

D1

What the account button opens

Options: the existing Profile sheet with a Challenges tile; or the Codex half-height menu; or a medium/large detent sheet with Profile below the fold.

Recommend the existing Profile. It keeps streak, points hero, best catch and laurels, and costs nothing to build.

D2

Live-challenge entry on Leaders

Toolbar flag only, or flag plus a live strip under the window switcher.

Recommend flag plus strip. The strip only exists while you are in a challenge.

D3

Late joins

Accept until the end with all in-window catches counting; or close joins at 50% elapsed; or count only catches after acceptance.

Recommend accept until the end, as you proposed. The join screen states it.

D4

Zero-point endings

Two or more participants, nobody scored: No Contest, or a shared win.

Recommend No Contest.

D5

Cancelling after start

Never; or creator only.

Recommend never. Leaving covers the creator's own exit.

D6

Creator leaves before start

Cancels the challenge; or the challenge continues without them.

Recommend cancels. An upcoming challenge with no creator is a zombie.

D7

Catch log fields

Model, rarity, points, time; or also operator and airline.

Recommend without operator. An airline plus a time can identify a flight, and you asked for no catch-identifying data.

D8

Validation verdict in challenges

Ignore (same as the global board); or exclude implausible catches in challenges only.

Recommend ignore in v1, watch the guardrail, and decide app-wide through the anti-cheat plan.

D9

Upload grace

15 minutes after the end; or none; or an hour.

Recommend 15 minutes. Long enough for a cellular retry, short enough that results feel immediate.

D10

Trophy

One hard trophy, Ace: win five challenges with at least two other spotters; or none; or also an easy participation trophy.

Recommend Ace only, in phase 3. Matches your hard-and-rare taste.

D11

Handle requirement

Required to create or join; or allowed anonymously with "anonymous spotter" rows.

Recommend required. Inline claim on the join sheet keeps it one screen.

D12

Challenge names

Free text with suggestion and profanity filter; or presets only.

Recommend free text with a prefilled suggestion.

D13

Kill switch

Server env flag with client fail-soft; or client-only build flag.

Recommend server flag. It also lets phase 0 ship with the tile present but hidden.

D14

Where phase 0 ships

A 1.1.x point release; or the first item of the v1.2 train.

Recommend the v1.2 train. A bar change with nothing new behind it is a strange point release.

D15

Limits

10 participants, 5 open created, 10 open participations, 10 creations a day; or looser.

Recommend these numbers. Raising them later is additive.

D16

Link domain and manual steps

tailspot.app/c/CODE with Associated Domains; or App Store links plus codes only.

Recommend tailspot.app links. Needs you to enable the capability; codes make the feature usable before that lands.
