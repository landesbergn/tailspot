Multi-catch bonus

Review of the 2026-11-15 build on PR #428. The screens are the real SwiftUI views rendered in the iOS 26 simulator with sample planes. The app half is on your phone. The server half is not deployed, so the leaderboard does not pay the bonus yet.

The rule

Catch two or more new planes with one press of the button and every plane in that press earns extra points on top of its own base.

  • The bonus adds to the base, the same way first-of-type and the guess bonus do. Bonuses stack and never multiply each other.
  • Only new planes count. A plane you already caught that is in the frame makes no catch, so a new plane plus a repeat is a single catch with no bonus.
  • Who gets into a press is unchanged under both catch rules, so the A/B is not disturbed.

The reveal card

Each plane's reveal shows the bonus as its own gold line in the points ledger, and the total includes it.

The summary after a multi-catch

One total for the press

The last page of a multi-catch adds the planes up. It now has a DOUBLE CATCH or TRIPLE CATCH line with the bonus for the whole press, and the total includes it.

In this example three planes were in the frame and one was already in the Hangar. Two were new, so it is a double: 25% of the two bases (50 and 10) is 13 + 3 = 16.

The same line appears on the Hangar row, the catch detail card, the share card and the weekly recap.

How the points get paid

  • The server decides. Every plane of one press already carries the same catch time, so the server counts how many different planes one phone caught at that instant. The app sends nothing new.
  • Because of that, builds already on people's phones earn the bonus as soon as the server is deployed. No app update is needed for the points, only for the ledger line.
  • Past multi-catches are paid too. A one-time re-score adds the bonus to every old press.
  • Deleting one plane of a double takes the bonus away from the other.

Until the server is deployed, the app runs ahead of the leaderboard. The reveal and the Hangar show the bonus; the leaderboard and your server total do not. That is expected for this test.

The wrench panel

One scrolling card

The panel had grown past the screen. Switches sat under the capture button and streak buttons were cut to one letter.

It is now one card that scrolls and ends above the capture button. The order is readouts, actions, streak tools, switches, then the nearby aircraft list. Every button shows its whole label.

Testing on your phone

  1. Open the wrench panel. Under SWITCHES, turn on New catch rule. It puts more planes in a press, so doubles are much easier to find. The test also works with it off.
  2. Go where two planes share the sky: an approach path with parallel runways, or a busy corridor. Both must be planes you have not caught.
  3. Frame both. Watch the button show ×2 and hold it steady for a second or two. Check: the count should not flicker back to a single catch while both planes are on screen.
  4. Press. Check: the reveal opens right away, with a page per plane, and each photo fills in after.
  5. On each plane's page, check for the gold DOUBLE CATCH line and a total of base plus bonus (the table at the top has the numbers).
  6. On the last page, check the summary has a DOUBLE CATCH line and the total adds up.
  7. In the Hangar, open one of the two planes. Check: the detail card shows the same line.
  8. Optional: catch one new plane together with one you already have. Check: it is a plain single catch with no bonus.

Worth noting when you report back: how the button felt just before the press, how long the reveal took to appear, and whether either plane was the wrong one.

Known gaps

  • Under the old catch rule, the 0.6 second hold can leave the button on ×2 briefly after your aim settles on one plane.
  • A press that straddles midnight can show a reveal page for a plane that did not save.
  • Each plane's photo search still decodes the photo again, so the photos of a triple arrive slower than they could.
  • The server does not check that the planes of a press could share one camera frame. A hand-made client could claim a double. It can already claim any catch.
